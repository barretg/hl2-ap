# Half-Life 2 Archipelago setup

## Requirements

- Half-Life 2 on Steam (the 20th anniversary update).
- Archipelago 0.6.7 or later, with `half_life_2.apworld` in its custom worlds.

## Installing the mod

1. Open the Archipelago Launcher and start the **Half-Life 2 Client**.
2. In the client, type `/install`. This installs the `hl2ap` mod as a Steam
   sourcemod; your Half-Life 2 install is never modified.
3. Restart Steam. **Half-Life 2 Archipelago** appears in your library.
4. On Linux only: in that entry's Properties > Compatibility, force a Proton
   version (Steam does not carry Half-Life 2's setting over).

## Playing

1. Connect the client to your room.
2. Launch Half-Life 2 Archipelago from Steam and start a New Game (or load a
   save). You are taken to the hub.
3. Type `!ap` in chat to see your chapters and `!warp <number>` to start one.

Keep the client running while you play: it carries items in and checks out.
`/uninstall` removes the mod again.
